01

Bracket outcome model

One eventual stop-or-target outcome per available session. At zero drift and cost, P(win) = stop / (stop + target). New targets get new probabilities—not reused historical wins.

μ and σ share an arbitrary diffusion-time unit. This model does not predict holding time, tick execution, or achievable market cash/day. The 70/30 split is over model paths, not historical data.

02

LucidFlex 50K · DLL off

Idealized zero-cost, immediate-settlement defaults match the research example. Fees are dated examples. One theoretical contract with adjustable dollar brackets; all payouts retained. The account engine still applies consistency, drawdown, payout rules and retries.

03

Search space

The initial policy has the same risk and target in every regime. “Funded build” means five winning days left before the first payout; other funded states use the fallback. The expanded set adds specific days-left, prior-payout and profit regimes, in first-match order. Search is heuristic, not proof of a global optimum.

04

Optimize and evaluate

The supplied $2,000/$1,500 → $2,000/$2,000 → $2,000/$150 example is evaluated only after selection as a reference. It is never inserted into the search. Repeatedly tuning to holdout results invalidates that holdout.
