Model notes

Stops and targets are ideal fills. Historical ratios stay fixed. Costs, payout processing and retries follow the configured scenario. Live handoff starts another paid evaluation under the same retry delay and wallet limits; it is not a failure. Exchange holidays, discretionary reviews and live-account value are not modeled. 04

Run & evaluate

Every complete window starts a fresh account and wallet. In fit mode, select on IS windows and report OOS windows. Incomplete tails are excluded; overlapping starts are not independent simulations.

IS-unvisited regimes retain their initial risk. OOS is a holdout only if it is not reused to tune the strategy or search. Custom objectives are available through the Python API.

Funding failure means unable to pay the next required account fee. The cash report uses the initial wallet and reported horizon; these settings do not change the optimization objective.

Multi-path and ultimate ruin analysis

All payouts remain available for future fees. After optimization, only OOS sessions seed the bootstrap. Ultimate ruin uses a separate approximation based on independent settled account cycles.
